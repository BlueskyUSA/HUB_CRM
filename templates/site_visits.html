Personal links (also your invitations while the site is private)

Make a link for one person or group, paste it into your own email or text, and see here whether they opened it and which pages they looked at. The link takes them to the page you pick. It shows that the link was opened, not who was holding the phone, so a forwarded link or a mail program that opens links by itself can look like a visit. While the site is private, opening one of these links is what lets someone in: it works for 90 days on their device, and turning the link off ends it. Tell people in your privacy statement that the site counts visits.
