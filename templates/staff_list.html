Administrators have the Owner’s powers when the Owner is away, except those in the last row and creating, changing or removing another Administrator. Leadership logins normally get “full access,” which switches on every general area at once (the “Yes” rows); the Owner can untick any of them, and the money, letters, Ambassadors and these two history pages are always separate ticks. Volunteer Team access is whatever the Owner ticks for the person (for example, a TA Team Captain is ticked for the Session Team Roster and the Trainee Roster). Accounting & Legal logins can only be given Accounting, Donations, Contracts and Contacts, and can look but not change anything except entering payments.

Add or remove a login: Contacts → Hub Login Logins are added and removed on the Contacts page (the Hub Login column). Here you choose what each person can open.
