HUB Access

The Owner and Administrators see this page{% if not session.is_owner %}, along with Leadership{% endif %}. It controls who can log in{% if session.is_owner %} and what each person can open{% else %}. Leadership can reset passwords for Volunteer Team accounts; the Owner handles everything else{% endif %}.

Administrators have the same Hub access as the Owner, with these exceptions. An Administrator cannot change the program code, transfer or change ownership, change the Owner's login or password, create, change or remove another Administrator, or open Owner's Corner (in the Administrator menu).

Leadership and Volunteer access is set on this page and depends on each person's role in the program. For example, a TA Team Captain can open the Session Team Roster and the Trainee Roster (Programs menu → Upcoming Sessions).

Add or remove a login: Contacts → Hub Login Logins are added and removed on the Contacts page (the Hub Login column). Here you choose what each person can open.
