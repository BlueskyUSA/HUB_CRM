Our Mission

Mission Statement

Reflect the love of God by providing a powerful, effective, and emotionally safe environment where individuals can identify and overcome life's challenges, discovering greater freedom, joy, peace, and love.

Organizational Goals

Build an enduring ministry that outlasts its current leadership by:

  1. Operating profitably, with sufficient reserves to ensure long-term sustainability.
  2. Ultimately licensing the ministry's intellectual property, software, equipment, and other essential assets to the next generation of leaders at no cost.

HUB — Goals & Objectives

Use automation to:

  1. Simplify administration and reduce operating costs.
  2. Expand marketing and enrollment through trained volunteer Guides and Sponsors.
  3. Streamline communication, registration, and participant follow-up.

Website — Goals & Objectives

  1. Appeal primarily to Generation X, while also welcoming Millennials and Baby Boomers.
  2. Communicate the value of experiential personal growth through contemporary design and compelling content.
  3. Encourage curiosity, inquiries, and enrollment.
