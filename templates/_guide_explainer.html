How to change an Ambassador

Who can do this: only the Owner and the Leadership accounts the Owner has chosen (HUB Access > Ambassadors). Everyone else can see who the Ambassadors are but cannot change them.

{% if has_guides_access %}

Change who is on the Ambassador team: open Manage the Ambassadors to add a new Ambassador or remove one. Only Ambassadors on the list receive new call requests, and each Ambassador belongs to one program group.

Hand one person to a different Ambassador: open Call requests. On any request an Ambassador has claimed, pick another Ambassador from the “Hand to…” list and click Hand over. The new Ambassador is emailed their own link and takes over from there. The person has already been told the first Ambassador's name, so you may want to let them know about the change.

{% else %}

You don't have this permission. To change the Ambassador team, or hand a person to a different Ambassador, ask the Owner.
