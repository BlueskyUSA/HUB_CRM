Discovery Legacy is a Texas nonprofit corporation that offers experiential weekend programs to help individuals and couples build self-awareness, communication and relationship skills.

Why this matters

Most of us were never taught how to understand ourselves or how to stay close to the people we love.

Discovery Legacy’s programs are three-day experiences, not lectures. They give individuals and couples the chance to slow down, look honestly at their own story, and practice new ways of connecting.

Our programs are educational. They are not counseling, therapy, or mental-health treatment.

Where your gift goes

Our goal is to build a foundation of about $100,000 for operating expenses and reserves, so that our first programs can run without debt and so that the work can endure and be passed on to the next team of leaders.

  • Operating expenses to open and run our first programs.
  • Reserves, so the programs are strong enough to last.

Our programs are led by trained volunteers, and we plan to publish a financial report every year so you can see how gifts were used.
