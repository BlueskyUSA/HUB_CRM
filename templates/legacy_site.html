Why this matters

Most of us were never taught how to truly understand ourselves, navigate life’s emotional challenges, or build deeply meaningful relationships with the people we love.

We learn how to earn a living, raise families, build careers, and meet life’s responsibilities. Yet many of us find ourselves searching for something more — greater freedom, peace, joy, purpose, and connection.

At Discovery Legacy, we believe that every human being has inherent value and that our past does not have to determine our future.

The Heart Behind Our Mission

Our desire is to reflect the love of God in a practical and meaningful way, creating an environment where people from all walks of life feel welcomed, valued, and accepted.

Through immersive, experiential seminars, we invite individuals and couples to explore their beliefs, recognize patterns in their lives, discover new perspectives, and develop healthier ways of relating to themselves and others.

We want people to experience the possibilities that emerge when they begin to live with greater awareness, personal responsibility, grace, and courage.

Looking Beyond Ourselves

Our objective for Discovery! is much more than conducting seminars. It is about building a program that will continue to serve people long after we are gone.

We want to create an organization that is spiritually grounded, financially responsible, technologically innovative, and equipped to empower the next generation of leaders.

We believe that what we build today can become a gift to generations we may never meet. That is why we are inviting you to become part of this story.

Where your gift goes

Our plan

Here is the path we intend to follow, from pledges to a program that endures. No agreement has been signed, and the details may change.

1. Establish a Foundation of Financial Support

Secure recurring monthly pledges from the Discovery! volunteer community totaling a minimum of $10,000 per month. No funds are required at this time; we are initially seeking nonbinding commitments of financial support.

This could be achieved through any combination of pledges, such as:

  • 100 volunteers pledging $100 per month.
  • 200 volunteers pledging $50 per month.
  • 400 volunteers pledging $25 per month.

Of course, in addition to monthly donations, one-time large donations would be ideal and welcomed to accelerate a payoff of the acquisition price.

Establishing this level of financial support will provide greater confidence in our ability to meet the acquisition obligation, fund operations, and build adequate financial reserves.

2. Finalize the Acquisition Agreement

Once sufficient pledges have been secured, finalize an agreement to license and acquire the Discovery! intellectual property (IP) and associated assets for a total purchase price of $250,000. The proposed acquisition terms are:

  • The purchase will be seller-financed through monthly payments of approximately $4,167 over five years.
  • Monthly payments will begin 90 days after the first Discovery! training is completed.
  • Ownership of the acquired intellectual property will remain with the current owner until the purchase price has been paid in full.
  • Upon full payment, Discovery Legacy will have acquired ownership of the intellectual property and associated assets covered by the agreement.
  • If a monthly payment is missed, Discovery Legacy will have 30 days to cure the default. Failure to do so will result in termination of the agreement and loss of the licensing rights necessary to continue operating Discovery! programs.

3. Secure Initial Startup Funding

After the acquisition and licensing agreement is signed, secure approximately $5,000 in short-term, non-interest-bearing loans from individuals willing to help with the initial organizational expenses. These funds will not be treated as tax-deductible charitable contributions and are intended to be repaid by Discovery Legacy as its financial resources permit. The expected use of these funds:

  • $4,000, legal expenses: attorney fees to prepare, file and process the application for federal tax-exempt recognition under Section 501(c)(3).
  • $1,000, startup expenses: reimbursement of documented software expenses, government filing fees and other organizational costs incurred to date.

4. Activate Monthly Financial Commitments

After the Section 501(c)(3) application is filed and the necessary banking and financial infrastructure is in place, begin collecting the monthly contributions that have been pledged. If the IRS approves the application with an effective date covering those contributions, they may qualify as tax-deductible charitable contributions, subject to applicable federal tax laws.

Until federal tax-exempt recognition has been obtained, contributors will be advised that the deductibility of their contributions is not assured. If the IRS denies the application, contributions may not qualify for a charitable tax deduction.

5. Establish Long-Term Financial Sustainability

Our ultimate objective is to establish a financially responsible and enduring program that:

  • Meets its acquisition and operating obligations.
  • Generates sufficient operating surpluses to support its programs.
  • Builds adequate financial reserves.
  • Reduces its dependence on charitable contributions over time, where possible.
  • Provides future generations of leadership with secure access to the intellectual property and resources necessary to continue the mission of Discovery!

Our goal is not simply to reopen Discovery! in North Texas, but to establish a program capable of serving individuals, couples, families and communities for generations to come.

  • Your gift goes only to Discovery Legacy. Never to the founders personally and never to {{ llc_name }}. Discovery Legacy will have its own bank account, books and records, kept by a bookkeeper and accountant.
  • The program rights belong to the LLC, and the nonprofit holds a license. The proposed license would give Discovery Legacy exclusive rights to run the programs in Dallas, Fort Worth and surrounding counties for an initial six-year term, renewing automatically after that. The LLC could end it or decline to renew only for stated causes, such as an unpaid fee after notice or unmet quality standards. It is designed to continue if the LLC is sold or if either founder dies or can no longer serve.
  • Discovery Legacy keeps its own money, donor and trainee records and program data. The LLC does not own them.
  • Any payment from Discovery Legacy to the LLC or to a founder is a related-party arrangement. It will be reviewed and approved by independent directors with outside support for its fairness, disclosed accurately to the IRS, and described on this page and in our annual financial reports. The terms have not been decided.

Establishing the Program

Initial priorities include legal formation and federal tax-exemption application, approved documented startup reimbursements, marketing, technology, insurance, facilities, supplies and volunteer support. Our first planned seminar, The Art of Us, is tentatively scheduled for February 12–14, 2027, subject to readiness.

Building Financial Strength and Sustainability

Historically, Discovery! programs have relied on community contributions. The former Dallas program was not self-sustaining through earned revenue in the available 2015–2018 financial records. Reported profitability in 2019 and early 2020 has not been verified. We believe The Art of Us, priced at $1,299 per couple, could help support our other programs if enrollment and costs meet expectations. Financial sustainability is a goal, not a guarantee.

Your Commitment Matters

Monthly support provides dependable operating resources. Larger one-time gifts help build the reserves that let the program endure. We intend to publish annual financial reports. Every commitment matters. Today we are asking for pledges only, not payments. Federal tax-exempt recognition has not yet been obtained.

About and transparency

Discovery Legacy was formed on November 20, 2026, as a Texas nonprofit corporation. We believe supporters deserve to understand how the organization is structured, how its resources are managed, and what we hope to achieve.

Our Organizational Structure

Discovery Legacy is the nonprofit operating entity responsible for seminars, community outreach, volunteers and the program’s mission. It will receive all gifts and program fees into its own bank account.

{{ llc_name }} is the proposed privately owned Texas company of Kent and Pamela Hafemann that would hold the acquired Discovery! rights, equipment and Hub software, and license them to Discovery Legacy for Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding counties. These arrangements have not been finalized. See the picture on the Where your gift goes page.

Because the founders lead Discovery Legacy and would own the LLC, any payment between the two is a related-party arrangement. Independent directors will review and approve it with outside support for its fairness, and we will disclose it in our annual financial reports. Our intention is to provide ongoing licensing without royalties or profit-based fees, subject to reimbursement of reasonable, documented costs and final agreements, unless independent directors approve otherwise.

The Discovery! name

Discovery Legacy intends to do business as Discovery! once it has acquired the program rights and has permission to use the name. Until then it operates under its legal name, Discovery Legacy.

Governance and Financial Accountability

Discovery Legacy is governed by a volunteer board and bylaws that include a conflict-of-interest policy. We intend to make these documents available upon request and publish annual financial reports. Related-party arrangements will require independent review and approval, including evaluation of fair value and the nonprofit’s long-term interests. Our books will be kept by a bookkeeper and accountant firm using QuickBooks Online.

Our goal is to equip future leaders with secure access to intellectual property, software, equipment and other essential resources. Through HUB automation, trained volunteer Ambassadors and Sponsors, streamlined communications and contemporary marketing, we hope to reduce overhead and expand enrollment. Financial sustainability is not guaranteed.

Federal Tax-Exempt Status

Discovery Legacy intends to apply for recognition under Section 501(c)(3). Federal tax-exempt recognition has not yet been obtained. We are not representing future contributions as tax-deductible. We are accepting nonbinding pledges only; no funds are being collected.
